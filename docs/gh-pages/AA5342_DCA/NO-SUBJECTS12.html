Remarks from a (past) European perspective , but I guess the same applies in the US.

Collecting data on incidents is the easiest part, Then you have to have a team looking at them , determine eventual patterns and make recommendations . Different sort of knowledge required, much more complex and will take time . . It then goes to the next level, : publishing those recommendations . That can be tricky and can also take time and can be amended by people above you pay grade. but it remains in the Investigation organization domain . The last level is implementation of the published recommendations , this goes outside the Incident investigation organization , and can be ignored ( which happens in the vast majority of cases) , or takes years to materialize.
Yes incident investigation is a very frustrating business.
