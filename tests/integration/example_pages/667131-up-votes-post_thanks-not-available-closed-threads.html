Paul,

Interesting observation, having just confirmed it now. It would be interesting to know what the [human] logic is for that move.

FWIW I have previously posted in this thread that the recent changes were a retrograde step for me. I used to find that the immediate 'length' of likes and who liked was useful towards 'grading' the validity of some statement or other; if the likes come from users I respect, or know have some implicit knowledge of a subject, then that adds weight to what iwas being said in the associated post (notwithstanding it may simply be an appreciation of humour or some other irrelevancy!

This and a few other things has led me to contemplate an 'extension' (for Firefox in my case) that might address certain 'issues' and enhance usability/readability. While it's probably something I could look at it'd probably take me months, if not years, whereas I suspect you could do it in 1/100th of the time .. do you have any interest in anything like that?

Otherwise I think the thread I mentioned above is useful to gauge the view of those who have to administer/moderate the new methodology. It's long been said that all the IB sites have to be somewhat homogeneous, but it doesn't seem that's universally applauded, and I am left to wonder if 'corporate' have any real idea of the audience for some of their sites... just so it's clear (since they're a very crude non-specific thing) my 'like' on this thread for Asturias56 is a measure of sympathy, and appreciation for their reasonable response(s).
